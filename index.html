Shopping FAQ

Find answers to the most common questions about our services, policies, and processes.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy on all unused items in original packaging. Once we receive your return, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method. Domestic orders include a prepaid return shipping label.

What if I receive a damaged item?

If you receive a damaged item, please contact our customer support team within 48 hours with photos of the damage. We'll arrange a free replacement or full refund immediately, no return shipment required.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3-7 business days within the continental US. Expedited shipping (1-3 business days) is available at checkout. All orders include tracking numbers sent via email once shipped.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes. We use 256-bit SSL encryption to protect all transactions. We are PCI DSS compliant and never store your full credit card information on our servers. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, and PayPal.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number and a link to track your package in real time. You can also track your order by logging into your account on our website.
